# Profile: concise

Shorten the prompt without losing any requirement.

## Rules
- Remove politeness, repetition, and motivational filler ("I would really appreciate…").
- Merge duplicate instructions; keep the stricter version.
- Prefer one clear verb over a phrase ("summarise", not "provide a summary of").
- Keep every constraint, placeholder, and output-format detail.
- Target 30–50% fewer words. If the prompt is already tight, say so and change little.
- Do not turn prose into terse fragments that change the tone the author wants (e.g. a mentor persona).

## Report back
Give the before and after word counts and list anything you removed that could matter.
